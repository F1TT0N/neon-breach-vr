Privacy policy

Neon Breach VR and its official website

Version 1.2 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the game, this website and support correspondence. Ordinary gameplay does not send your saves or movement history to Anwar Studio Official. The game still processes information locally, and visiting the website or contacting support involves the processing described below.

1. Who is responsible?

Anwar Studio Official develops and publishes Neon Breach VR and is responsible for its own processing described in this policy. For privacy questions and requests, contact psopala93@gmail.com. Meta and the providers identified below also process information for their own services under their respective privacy notices.

2. Information used on your headset

VR input: the game uses headset and controller position, orientation, tracking status and button input to render your view, aim and move. It does not record a continuous movement history or use this input to identify you. We do not receive a recording of your physical movements or a map of your room.

Local progress and preferences: the game saves mission progress, inventory, weapon upgrades, completion records, elapsed play time, difficulty, the character's position and viewing direction in the game, and settings such as audio, graphics, dominant hand and height adjustments. Save files also contain a save time and game/content version. This information enables manual saves, automatic checkpoints and resuming your campaign. It remains in the application's local storage; an in-game position is not a GPS location.

Local diagnostics: engine or system logs may contain errors, game events and device, operating-system or rendering information. The game does not automatically upload these logs to us. We can receive a diagnostic file only if you choose to send it, for example with a support email.

3. What the game does not collect

The game has no developer account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, automatic crash-report upload, multiplayer chat or developer-hosted save service. Its own code does not request camera images, microphone recordings, contacts, precise geographic location, your Meta profile or payment-card details. It does not use eye or face tracking for identification or behavioural profiling.

The Android application includes Internet permission, but the current game has no developer telemetry-upload or online-gameplay service. The core campaign works offline. Headset software, store services and optional platform backups operate separately.

4. Website visits and cookies

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. Delivering pages, images, videos and downloads also involves technical request information, such as the requested resource, browser/device information, request time and connection or error information. GitHub and its infrastructure providers process necessary information to deliver and protect their services. You do not need a GitHub account or a sign-in to read this website or this policy.

Our website code contains no advertising cookies, analytics pixels, contact forms, third-party video embeds or external fonts. It does not set optional cookies or create a visitor profile in browser storage. The trailer and images are served by the website's host. Selecting an email link opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically. These statements about our code do not mean the hosting platform processes no visitor data. See GitHub's privacy statement. External links lead to services with their own privacy policies.

5. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any sender name, message, communication metadata and attachments you choose to provide. A screenshot, save file, recording or diagnostic log may itself contain information about you. We use this material to answer the request, investigate the reported problem and provide related follow-up. Providing it is voluntary and is not required to play; without enough information we may be unable to investigate your issue.

Our published support address uses Gmail, so Google processes the correspondence to provide the email service. See Google's privacy policy. Please send only information relevant to the problem, and do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal information.

6. Sharing and platform services

Support correspondence is handled by the people responsible for answering it and the email service needed to deliver and store it. Website requests are handled by the hosting and access services. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary and lawful to address a specific security incident or legal claim. We do not sell personal information, use support messages for targeted advertising or make automated decisions about players with legal or similarly significant effects.

Meta independently operates the store, purchases, headset software and platform services. Its optional backup services may copy or restore application data according to your platform settings. We do not operate those services or receive your payment-card details. For Meta's processing, controls and deletion options, see Meta's privacy policy and the privacy and backup settings for your Meta account and headset.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies to personal information we process, information necessary to deliver the gameplay and save functionality you request is processed for the performance of the service contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)). Support correspondence and necessary website/security administration rely on our legitimate interests in assisting players, operating the website and protecting the service, subject to your rights (Article 6(1)(f)). Information that must be kept or disclosed to meet a legal duty is processed to comply with that obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). We do not use gameplay information to build advertising profiles.

8. How long information is kept

VR input: used during gameplay rather than retained as a tracking-history database. A save may retain the character's current in-game position and viewing direction as described above.

Saves and settings: remain on your headset until the relevant data is overwritten or removed. Starting a new campaign may replace an automatic checkpoint; it does not necessarily remove every manual save, record or setting. Local logs remain subject to the headset's or application's storage and log-management behaviour.

Support correspondence: retained for the time reasonably needed to resolve the request and complete necessary follow-up. Relevant information may be retained longer for an unresolved dispute, a specific legal claim or a legal retention requirement. The criteria are the status and nature of the issue, the need for related follow-up and applicable legal obligations; there is no single fixed period for every type of request.

Provider records and backups: hosting logs, email-service copies and Meta backups have provider-specific retention rules. We do not set those providers' independent retention periods. Their notices and controls are linked above.

9. Deleting information and exercising your rights

On your headset: use the platform's application-data removal option where available, or uninstall the game to remove its local application data. This can erase your progress and preferences. Review cloud-backup settings separately: a backup may survive uninstallation or restore data when the game is reinstalled. We cannot remotely delete files held only on your headset or remove backups controlled by your platform account.

Information you sent us: email psopala93@gmail.com with the subject “Neon Breach VR privacy request”. State whether you want access, correction or deletion and identify the relevant correspondence. Contacting us from the same email address helps locate it. We may request proportionate additional information if necessary to verify a request; please do not send identity documents unless specifically needed. We will explain if a lawful exception prevents deletion of some information.

Depending on your location and the applicable conditions, you may also have rights to restriction, objection, data portability and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. You can complain to the data-protection authority in your place of residence, work or the alleged infringement. For requests governed by the GDPR, we respond without undue delay and normally within one month. A permitted extension of up to two additional months may be necessary for complex or numerous requests; we will explain it within the initial month. Other applicable laws may set different requirements.

10. International processing and security

Email, hosting and platform providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their linked privacy notices explain their international processing and applicable safeguards, such as recognised transfer mechanisms where required. You may contact us for information about the services used for your request. We do not claim that all website or support data stays within one country.

The website uses HTTPS, while local saves rely on the headset's application-storage protections. Protect access to your headset and email account, and avoid including unnecessary personal information in support attachments.

11. Young players

Neon Breach VR is intended for a mature audience and contains violent and suggestive fictional content. Follow the store's assigned age rating and the headset platform's age requirements. We do not knowingly seek children's personal information through the game. Contact us if a child has sent personal information to support so we can review and address it.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its revision date when our data practices change, including before introducing developer accounts, analytics or other new online features. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially different processing. Previous versions are retained and can be requested at the contact address above.
