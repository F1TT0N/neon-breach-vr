Reporting a problem

Include the game version, headset model, level name and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are helpful. Please avoid sending passwords, account credentials or unrelated personal details.

Controls and comfort

Use the controller ray and trigger to choose menu items. A new mission asks you to select difficulty and adjust HUD height. The Controls page shows the current controller layout; comfort settings include seated or standing play, height and turning options.

Press and creators

Use the press kit for approved title art, actual game screenshots and a fact sheet. Identify the game as Neon Breach VR and the studio as Anwar Studio Official. Please distinguish promotional illustrations from gameplay captures.
